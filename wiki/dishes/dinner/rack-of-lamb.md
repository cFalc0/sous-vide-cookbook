# Rack of Lamb

Elegant, restaurant-quality rack with edge-to-edge medium-rare perfection. Sous vide at 130°F delivers the pink, juicy texture that traditional roasting often overcooks.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 rack of lamb (8 bones, Frenched)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper

---

## Steps

1. Preheat water bath to 130°F (54°C) for medium-rare.
2. Mix olive oil, garlic, rosemary, salt, and pepper in a small bowl.
3. Coat rack evenly with the herb mixture.
4. Place in a zipper-lock bag, seal using water displacement method.
5. Submerge in water bath and cook for 2-3 hours.
6. Remove from bag, pat extremely dry with paper towels.
7. Heat a cast-iron skillet over highest heat with 1 tbsp oil.
8. Sear meat side for 45-60 seconds until deep brown crust forms.
9. Rest for 5 minutes, then slice between bones and serve.

---

## Time and Temperature

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Temperature | 130°F / 54°C |
| Time | 2-3 hrs |
| Difficulty | 3/5 |

---

## Why Sous Vide Works Here

Rack of lamb is expensive and unforgiving — one minute too long in the oven and it's overcooked. Sous vide holds the meat at exactly 130°F, so every chop is medium-rare from bone to center. The sear adds the Maillard crust without cooking further.

---

## Tips

- French the bones before cooking for elegant presentation
- Sear bones-side first for 30 seconds for rendered fat flavor
- Rest 5 minutes before slicing to keep juices in
- Serve with mint pesto or red wine reduction
- Can be cooked from frozen by adding 30 minutes to cook time
