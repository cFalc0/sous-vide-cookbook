## Why Sous Vide for Dinner

Dinner is when you want to impress. Sous vide delivers:

- **Consistent results** — Edge-to-edge perfection every time
- **Complex flavors** — Long cooks develop deep tastes
- **Make-ahead ease** — Cook earlier, sear before serving

---

## Tips

- Combine sous vide with other techniques (searing, roasting) for contrast
- Use wine, herbs, and aromatics in the bag for built-in flavor
- Rest meats 5-10 minutes after searing for juicier results
- Plan your timing backwards from when you want to serve
