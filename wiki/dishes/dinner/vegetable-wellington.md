# Vegetable Wellington

Creative vegetarian centerpiece with mushrooms, spinach, and goat cheese wrapped in flaky pastry. Sous vide pre-cooks the filling at 183°F, ensuring everything is perfectly tender before the pastry bake.

---

## Ingredients

### For the Filling
- 1 lb mixed mushrooms (cremini, shiitake, portobello), diced
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 4 oz goat cheese, crumbled
- 1 shallot, minced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp fresh thyme
- Salt and pepper to taste

### For Assembly
- 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed
- 1 egg, beaten (for egg wash)
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard

---

## Steps

1. Preheat water bath to 183°F (84°C).
2. In a skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add shallot and garlic, cook 2 minutes.
3. Add mushrooms, thyme, salt, and pepper. Cook until moisture evaporates, about 8 minutes.
4. Add spinach and cook until wilted, 1 minute. Remove from heat, let cool completely.
5. Stir in goat cheese, then place mixture in a zipper-lock bag.
6. Seal using water displacement method, submerge and cook for 1.5-2 hours.
7. Remove from bag, drain excess liquid, let cool to room temperature.
8. Roll puff pastry on floured surface to 12x10 inches.
9. Spread Dijon mustard in center, add filling in a log shape leaving 1 inch border.
10. Brush edges with egg wash, fold pastry over filling, seal edges.
11. Brush top with egg wash, score decoratively, bake at 400°F for 30-35 minutes until golden.
12. Rest 10 minutes before slicing and serving.

## Why Sous Vide Works Here

Vegetable wellingtons often fail because the filling is undercooked while the pastry burns. Sous vide pre-cooks the mushrooms and spinach to perfect tenderness at 183°F, so the pastry only needs to bake until golden. The filling is evenly cooked and deeply flavored.

---

## Tips

- Squeeze out all moisture from cooked filling before wrapping
- Can be assembled ahead and refrigerated for up to 24 hours before baking
- Use a mix of mushrooms for complex flavor
- Serve with red wine reduction or garlic aioli
- Wrap tightly to prevent pastry from separating during baking
