# Pork Chops

Classic pork chops with juicy, pink interior that the USDA now says is safe. Sous vide at 140°F keeps the meat moist while eliminating the grey, dry texture of traditional cooking.

---

## Ingredients

- 2 bone-in pork chops (1.5 inches thick)
- 1 tbsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 2 sprigs fresh sage
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil (for searing)

---

## Steps

1. Preheat water bath to 140°F (60°C) for juicy, medium doneness.
2. Season pork chops generously with salt and pepper on both sides.
3. Place chops in a zipper-lock bag with sage sprigs.
4. Seal using water displacement method, submerge in water bath.
5. Cook for 1-4 hours — the time affects texture more than doneness.
6. Remove from bag, pat completely dry with paper towels.
7. Heat oil in cast-iron skillet over highest heat.
8. Sear chops 1-2 minutes per side until golden brown crust forms.
9. Rest 5 minutes, then serve with applesauce or pan sauce.

---

| Parameter | Value |

---

## Why Sous Vide Works Here

Pork chops were traditionally overcooked to 160°F+, resulting in dry, grey meat. Modern guidelines say 145°F is safe, and sous vide at 140°F delivers juicy, pink chops that are impossibly moist. The long cook window (1-4 hours) makes timing stress-free.

---

## Tips

- 140°F for juicy pink, 150°F for traditional doneness
- Thicker chops (2+ inches) benefit from the full 4 hours
- Apple, mustard, or herb finishes pair perfectly
- Can be cooked from frozen by adding 30 minutes
- Save bag juices to make quick pan sauce with wine and butter
