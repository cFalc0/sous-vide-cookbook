# Sous Vide Burger

Juicy, medium-rare burgers with edge-to-edge pink perfection. Sous vide at 130°F delivers restaurant-quality results that grilling cannot match.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef (80/20 or 85/15 blend)
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil (for searing)
- 4 burger buns
- Lettuce, tomato, onion (for serving)

---

## Steps

1. Form beef into 4 patties, about 1/2 inch thick, with a slight depression in the center.
2. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
3. Place patties in a zipper-lock bag in a single layer.
4. Seal using water displacement method.
5. Preheat water bath to 130°F (54°C) for medium-rare.
6. Submerge bag and cook for 1 hour.
7. Remove patties, pat extremely dry with paper towels.
8. Heat oil in cast-iron skillet over highest heat.
9. Sear patties 45-60 seconds per side until deep brown crust forms.
10. Toast buns, assemble burgers with desired toppings.

---

## Time and Temperature

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Temperature | 130°F / 54°C |
| Time | 1 hr |
| Difficulty | 2/5 |

---

## Why Sous Vide Works Here

Burgers overcook in seconds on the grill, going from juicy to dry. Sous vide holds the meat at exactly 130°F, so every bite is medium-rare and juicy. The sear only adds the crust you crave.

---

## Tips

- 130°F = medium-rare, 140°F = medium, 150°F = medium-well
- Do not cook below 130°F for longer than 2.5 hours
- Mix cheese into the patty before cooking for inside melts
- Cook patties from frozen by adding 30 minutes to cook time
- Hold at temperature for up to 4 hours before searing
