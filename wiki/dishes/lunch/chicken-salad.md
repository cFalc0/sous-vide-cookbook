# Chicken Salad

Perfectly cooked, juicy chicken breast that transforms into the best chicken salad. Sous vide at 145°F ensures the meat stays moist even when mixed with dressing.

---

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken
- 2 boneless chicken breasts (6-8 oz each)
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 2 sprigs fresh tarragon (optional)

### For the Salad
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 celery stalk, diced
- 1/4 red onion, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Salt and pepper to taste

---

## Steps

1. Preheat water bath to 145°F (63°C).
2. Season chicken breasts with salt and pepper.
3. Place in zipper-lock bag with tarragon sprigs.
4. Seal using water displacement method.
5. Submerge and cook for 1.5-4 hours.
6. Remove chicken, pat dry, and let cool to room temperature.
7. Dice chicken into 1/2-inch cubes.
8. In a bowl, whisk mayonnaise, mustard, and lemon juice.
9. Add chicken, celery, onion, and dill. Toss to coat.
10. Season with salt and pepper, serve chilled.

---

## Time and Temperature

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Temperature | 145°F / 63°C |
| Time | 1.5-4 hrs |
| Difficulty | 2/5 |

---

## Why Sous Vide Works Here

Chicken breast in salad often turns dry and chalky. Sous vide at 145°F keeps the meat juicy even after cooling and mixing with dressing. The long cook time also ensures full pasteurization.

---

## Tips

- Cook chicken breasts in batches, refrigerate for up to 4 days
- Add halved grapes or diced apple for sweetness
- Use Greek yogurt instead of mayo for lighter version
- Toast pecans or walnuts add great crunch
- Make ahead — flavors improve after 2 hours in refrigerator
