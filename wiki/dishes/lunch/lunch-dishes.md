## Why Sous Vide for Lunch

Lunch should be satisfying but not heavy. Sous vide helps:

- **Meal prep** — Cook multiple portions on Sunday
- **Food safety** — Hold at temp until ready to eat
- **Texture control** — Never overcooked salad proteins

---

## Tips

- Cook proteins in batches, sear fresh when assembling
- Store cooked proteins in refrigerator for up to 4 days
- Use the same water bath for multiple lunch components
- Prep grains and vegetables alongside proteins
