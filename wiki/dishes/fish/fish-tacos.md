# Fish Tacos

These vibrant sous vide fish tacos feature flaky, moist white fish (cod or halibut) cooked to 130°F, paired with a crunchy cilantro-lime slaw and warm corn tortillas. Sous vide eliminates the risk of overcooking lean white fish, delivering a firm, flaky texture that holds up perfectly to bold taco toppings without drying out.

---

## Ingredients

### For the Fish
- 2 white fish fillets (cod or halibut, 1 lb total, 1 inch thick)
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

### For the Cilantro Lime Slaw
- 2 cups green cabbage, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 2 scallions, sliced
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/4 tsp salt

### For Serving
- 8 corn tortillas, warmed
- 1 avocado, sliced
- Lime wedges
- Hot sauce (optional)

---

## Steps

1. Preheat sous vide water bath to 130°F (54°C).
2. In a small bowl, mix chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, salt, pepper, and olive oil to form a paste.
3. Rub spice paste evenly over all sides of fish fillets.
4. Place fillets in a zipper-lock bag, seal using water displacement method, and submerge in water bath.
5. Cook for 30-45 minutes for 1-inch fillets.
6. While fish cooks, prepare slaw: toss cabbage, cilantro, scallions, lime juice, olive oil, and salt in a bowl, refrigerate until ready to use.
7. Remove fish from bag, flake into large chunks using a fork.
8. Warm tortillas in a dry skillet over medium heat for 30 seconds per side.
9. Assemble tacos: spread slaw on tortillas, top with flaked fish, avocado slices, and lime juice.
10. Serve immediately with hot sauce and extra lime wedges.

## Why Sous Vide Works Here

Lean white fish like cod and halibut dry out quickly with traditional high-heat cooking, but 130°F delivers a firm, flaky texture that retains moisture. Sous vide cooks the fish evenly from edge to edge, so every bite is tender and flaky, perfect for holding up to crunchy slaw and bright taco toppings.

---

## Tips

- Use halibut for a firmer texture, or cod for a more delicate flake.
- Sear flaked fish in a hot skillet for 1 minute before assembling tacos for extra texture.
- Add diced mango or pickled red onion to the slaw for extra sweetness or tang.
- Make a chipotle crema by mixing sour cream with minced chipotle peppers for a spicy topping.
