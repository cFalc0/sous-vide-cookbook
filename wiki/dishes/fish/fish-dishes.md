## Why Sous Vide for Fish

Fish overcooks in seconds at high heat. Sous vide changes the game:

- **Salmon** — 115°F: buttery; 130°F: flaky
- **Tuna** — 110°F: sashimi-style tataki
- **White fish** — 130°F: firm, flaky, never dry
- **Fat content matters** — Black cod (15% fat) vs cod (1-2% fat)

---

## Tips

- Dry fish thoroughly before searing
- Use sushi-grade for temperatures below 130°F
- Chill after bath for easier handling
- Creative marinades penetrate deeply during cook
