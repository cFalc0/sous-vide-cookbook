# Miso Black Cod

This sweet-savory Japanese-inspired dish features rich, buttery black cod infused with a glossy white miso glaze, finished under the broiler. Sous vide at 104°F preserves the fish's high natural fat content, delivering a silky, sashimi-like texture while allowing the miso marinade to penetrate deeply.

---

## Ingredients

- 4 black cod (sablefish) fillets (6 oz each, skin-on)
- 1/4 cup white (shiro) miso paste
- 2 tbsp mirin
- 2 tbsp sake
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp freshly grated ginger
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- Toasted sesame seeds (for garnish)
- 1 scallion, thinly sliced (for garnish)

---

## Steps

1. In a small saucepan over medium heat, whisk together miso, mirin, sake, brown sugar, ginger, and sesame oil until smooth and slightly thickened, about 5 minutes. Let cool completely.
2. Place black cod fillets in a shallow dish, coat evenly with cooled miso marinade, cover, and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or up to 48 hours for deeper flavor.
3. Preheat sous vide water bath to 104°F (40°C).
4. Remove fillets from marinade, wipe off excess (reserve marinade for glazing), and place in a vacuum-sealable bag.
5. Seal bag using water displacement method, then submerge in water bath.
6. Cook for 45-60 minutes for 1-inch fillets.
7. Preheat broiler to high, or heat a kitchen torch.
8. Remove fillets from bag, pat gently dry, and place on a foil-lined baking sheet.
9. Brush reserved miso marinade over fillets, then broil 6 inches from heat for 1-2 minutes or torch until glaze is bubbly and caramelized.
10. Garnish with sesame seeds and scallion, serve with steamed rice.

## Why Sous Vide Works Here

Black cod's high fat content (15% compared to 1-2% for lean white fish) makes it forgiving, but 104°F preserves its delicate, buttery texture better than higher heat. Sous vide ensures the fish cooks evenly without drying, while the long marinade time allows the sweet miso glaze to penetrate deep into the flesh for restaurant-quality flavor.

---

## Tips

- Marinate fish for 24-48 hours for the most intense miso flavor, as traditional Nobu-style recipes call for.
- Handle cooked fish gently, as it will be very soft at 104°F.
- Substitute regular cod if black cod is unavailable, but reduce cook time by 10 minutes as it is leaner.
- Add a splash of rice vinegar to the miso marinade for extra brightness.
