# Brisket

Brisket is a tough, working muscle from the chest of the cow that requires long cooking to break down its dense connective tissue. Sous vide transforms this budget-friendly cut into tender, juicy meat with a consistent texture throughout, eliminating the expertise traditionally required for barbecue brisket.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 brisket flat or point cut (4 to 6 pounds)
- Salt (kosher or sea salt)
- Coarsely ground black pepper
- Optional: 1/4 teaspoon liquid smoke for barbecue flavor

---

## Steps

1. Preheat your sous vide precision cooker to 155°F (68°C) for a traditional, tender brisket texture.
2. Season the brisket generously with salt and pepper on all sides.
3. Place the brisket in a large vacuum-sealable bag (you may need to cut it into smaller pieces to fit).
4. Remove air using a vacuum sealer, ensuring a tight seal around the meat.
5. Submerge the sealed bag in the water bath and cook for 24 to 36 hours.
6. Remove the bag from the water and take out the brisket.
7. Pat the brisket dry with paper towels and let it cool to room temperature.
8. Preheat an oven to 300°F (150°C) or prepare a grill for indirect heat.
9. Rub additional salt and pepper into the surface of the brisket.
10. Roast in the oven for 2 hours or smoke on a grill at 275°F to 300°F for 3 hours until a dark bark forms.
11. Rest the brisket for 30 minutes, then slice thinly against the grain.

## Why Sous Vide Works Here

Brisket contains large amounts of collagen and connective tissue that require extended cooking at precise temperatures to become tender. Sous vide allows you to hold the meat at the ideal temperature for 24 to 72 hours without drying out, breaking down tough fibers while keeping the meat moist. This method removes the constant monitoring required in traditional barbecue and guarantees consistent results.

---

## Tips

- At 135°F for 36 to 72 hours, the brisket will have a firm, steak-like texture that can be sliced.
- At 155°F for 24 to 36 hours, the brisket becomes tender with a traditional barbecue texture.
- After the sous vide step, the brisket can be refrigerated for up to 1 week before finishing.
- Always slice brisket against the grain for maximum tenderness.
- The finishing step (oven or grill) is essential to develop the flavorful crust and bark.
