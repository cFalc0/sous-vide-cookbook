## Why Sous Vide for Red Meat

Red meat varies wildly in fat content and toughness. Sous vide handles both extremes:

- **Tender cuts** — Ribeye, filet: edge-to-edge perfect doneness
- **Tough cuts** — Short ribs, brisket: collagen → gelatin over 24-72 hrs
- **Temperature precision** — 1°F changes doneness noticeably

---

## Tips

- Thickness drives minimum time, not weight
- Below 130°F: max 4 hours for food safety
- Sear immediately before serving for best crust
- Rest 5-10 minutes after searing
