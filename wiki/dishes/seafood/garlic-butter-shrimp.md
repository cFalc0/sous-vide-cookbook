# Garlic Butter Shrimp

Plump, snappy shrimp cooked to tender perfection at 145°F, then tossed in a bright garlic butter sauce. Sous vide eliminates the guesswork, delivering consistently opaque, juicy shrimp that never turns rubbery.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 lb large shrimp (16-20 count), peeled and deveined
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- Lemon wedges (for serving)

---

## Steps

1. Preheat sous vide water bath to 145°F (63°C).
2. Season shrimp with salt and pepper in a bowl.
3. Place shrimp in a single layer in a zipper-lock bag.
4. Add 1 tbsp butter (cut into small pieces) and half the minced garlic.
5. Seal using water displacement method, ensuring shrimp are in one layer.
6. Submerge bag in water bath and cook for 20-30 minutes.
7. Meanwhile, melt remaining butter in a skillet over medium heat.
8. Add remaining garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant (do not brown).
9. Remove shrimp from bag, pat dry, and add to garlic butter.
10. Toss 1-2 minutes until shrimp are coated and warm.
11. Drizzle with lemon juice, garnish with parsley, serve with lemon wedges.

---

## Time and Temperature

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Temperature | 145°F / 63°C |
| Time | 20-30 min |
| Difficulty | 2/5 |

---

## Why Sous Vide Works Here

Shrimp overcooks in 30 seconds of traditional high-heat cooking, turning rubbery and tough. Sous vide holds them at exactly 145°F, ensuring plump, snappy texture every time. The gentle heat preserves their natural sweetness while the butter sauce adds richness without overcooking.

---

## Tips

- Use extra-large or jumbo shrimp for best results — smaller ones can overcook faster
- Do not exceed 30 minutes or shrimp may become too firm
- Pat shrimp very dry before adding to garlic butter to avoid a watery sauce
- Add a pinch of red pepper flakes to the garlic butter for heat
- Reserve a tablespoon of the bag juices to add to the butter sauce for extra flavor
