## Why Sous Vide for Seafood

Seafood turns rubbery in seconds of traditional cooking:

- **Shrimp** — 145°F: plump, snappy; overcooks → rubbery
- **Scallops** — 130°F: opaque, buttery; sear for Maillard crust
- **Lobster** — 140°F: sweet, tender; traditional methods overcook
- **Squid** — 140°F: buttery tender; traditionally chewy

---

## Tips

- Buy "dry" scallops (not treated) for better searing
- Pat seafood extremely dry before searing
- Do not exceed recommended times
- Creative presentations: tacos, pasta, salads
