# Seared Scallops

Buttery, restaurant-quality scallops with a golden crust and creamy interior, finished with a bright lemon beurre blanc. Sous vide at 130°F ensures they're perfectly opaque throughout, then a quick sear adds the Maillard crust that makes scallops unforgettable.

---

## Ingredients

- 8-10 large sea scallops (U-10 size, about 1 lb)
- 2 tbsp high smoke-point oil (avocado or grapeseed)
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 shallot, minced
- 1/4 cup dry white wine
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp fresh chives, chopped
- Salt and pepper to taste

---

## Steps

1. Preheat sous vide water bath to 130°F (54°C).
2. Pat scallops completely dry with paper towels — moisture prevents searing.
3. Season scallops with salt and pepper.
4. Place scallops in a single layer in a zipper-lock bag.
5. Add 1 tbsp butter (cut into small pieces) to the bag.
6. Seal using water displacement method, removing all air.
7. Submerge bag in water bath and cook for 20-30 minutes.
8. While scallops cook, make beurre blanc: melt 1 tbsp butter in a small saucepan, add shallot and cook 2 minutes.
9. Add wine and lemon juice, simmer until reduced by half (about 3 minutes).
10. Remove scallops from bag, pat extremely dry with paper towels.
11. Heat oil in a cast-iron skillet over highest heat until smoking.
12. Sear scallops 45-60 seconds per side until deep golden crust forms.
13. Plate scallops, drizzle with beurre blanc, garnish with chives.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Temperature | 130°F / 54°C |

---

## Why Sous Vide Works Here

Scallops turn from translucent to opaque at exactly 130°F. Traditional pan-searing often overcooks the centers while developing the crust. Sous vide ensures the entire scallop is perfectly cooked, so the sear only needs to create the crust without cooking further.

---

## Tips

- Buy "dry" scallops (not treated with preservatives) — they sear better and taste sweeter
- Remove the small side muscle (abductor) before cooking for better texture
- Chill scallops in fridge 10 minutes after sous vide for easier handling
- Do not crowd the pan — sear in batches if needed
- Serve immediately while crust is crisp
