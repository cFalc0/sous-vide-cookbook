# Lobster Tails

Luxurious lobster tails with tender, snappy meat and a rich garlic-herb butter. Sous vide at 140°F delivers perfect texture that traditional steaming often overcooks.

---

## Ingredients

- 4 lobster tails (5-6 oz each)
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tbsp fresh chives, chopped (for garnish)

---

## Steps

1. Preheat sous vide water bath to 140°F (60°C).
2. Using kitchen shears, cut through the top shell lengthwise, stop before the tail fan.
3. Gently pull meat up through the cut, resting it on top of the shell (butterfly style).
4. Season lobster meat with salt, paprika, and cayenne.
5. Place lobster tails in a zipper-lock bag with butter and garlic.
6. Seal using water displacement method, ensuring butter coats each tail.
7. Submerge in water bath and cook for 25-35 minutes.
8. Remove from bag, pat very dry with paper towels.
9. Heat a skillet over high heat or use a kitchen torch.
10. Sear lobster meat 30-45 seconds until lightly golden.
11. Drizzle with lemon juice, garnish with chives, serve immediately.

---

## Time and Temperature

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Temperature | 140°F / 60°C |
| Time | 25-35 min |
| Difficulty | 3/5 |

---

## Why Sous Vide Works Here

Lobster meat goes from tender to rubbery in just 2-3°F. Traditional boiling or steaming often overcooks the delicate meat. Sous vide holds the tails at exactly 140°F, ensuring snappy, juicy texture throughout every bite.

---

## Tips

- Choose cold-water lobster tails for sweeter, firmer meat
- Butterfly the tails for even cooking and elegant presentation
- Reserve the melted butter from the bag to drizzle over the finished dish
- A quick torch sear adds smoky notes without overcooking
- Serve with drawn butter and lemon wedges for the full experience
