# Squid Ink Pasta

Creative surf-and-turf dish featuring tender calamari rings served over pasta with a dramatic black ink sauce. Sous vide at 140°F transforms typically chewy squid into buttery, tender morsels that elevate any pasta dish.

---

## Ingredients

### For the Calamari
- 1 lb fresh squid, cleaned, bodies cut into rings
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, sliced
- 1 tsp lemon zest
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

### For the Pasta
- 12 oz fettuccine or linguine
- 2 tbsp squid ink
- 1/4 cup white wine
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 lemon, wedged (for serving)

---

## Steps

1. Preheat sous vide water bath to 140°F (60°C).
2. In a bowl, toss squid rings with olive oil, garlic, lemon zest, salt, and pepper.
3. Place squid in a zipper-lock bag with all marinade.
4. Seal using water displacement method, removing all air.
5. Submerge in water bath and cook for 45-60 minutes.
6. While squid cooks, boil pasta in salted water until al dente.
7. Drain pasta, reserving 1/2 cup pasta water.
8. In a large skillet, melt butter over medium heat, add wine and squid ink.
9. Simmer 2 minutes, then add cooked pasta and toss with pasta water as needed.
10. Remove squid from bag, pat dry, and add to pasta for 1 minute to warm through.
11. Garnish with parsley and lemon wedges, serve immediately.

---

## Time and Temperature

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Temperature | 140°F / 60°C |

| Difficulty | 3/5 |

---

## Why Sous Vide Works Here

Calamari is notorious for going from tender to rubbery in seconds of traditional high-heat cooking. Sous vide at 140°F breaks down the proteins slowly, transforming chewy squid into buttery, tender rings that hold up beautifully in pasta dishes.

---

## Tips

- Clean squid thoroughly — remove inner cartilage and outer membrane
- Serve over pasta, in salads, or as a standalone appetizer with aioli
- Add chili flakes to the pasta for a spicy kick
- For dramatic presentation, drizzle extra squid ink over the finished dish
- Pair with a crisp white wine like Pinot Grigio
