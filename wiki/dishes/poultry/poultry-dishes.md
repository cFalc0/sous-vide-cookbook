## Why Sous Vide for Poultry

Poultry is unforgiving with traditional methods:

- **Chicken breast** — 145°F: juicy; 165°F (traditional): dry
- **Dark meat** — Higher temps break down collagen, stays moist
- **Whole chicken** — Even cooking, no dry breast while thighs finish
- **Duck** — Render fat separately, keep meat pink and juicy

---

## Tips

- Chicken breast: 145°F for 1.5-4 hrs (pasteurizes safely)
- Chicken thighs: 4-8 hrs for pull-off-the-bone
- Whole chicken: Spatchcock for even cooking
- Duck: Pre-sear skin to render fat, then sous vide
