# Chicken Thighs

Sous vide chicken thighs turn rich, collagen-heavy dark meat into exceptionally tender and juicy results that rival your favorite restaurant preparations. The precise temperature control lets you choose between sliceable thighs or fall-off-the-bone tenderness by simply adjusting the cooking time.

---

## Ingredients

- 4 bone-in or boneless chicken thighs (with skin preferred)
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme or rosemary (optional)
- 1 clove garlic, smashed (optional)
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil (for searing)

---

## Steps

1. Preheat water bath to 165°F (74°C) using a sous vide circulator.
2. Season chicken thighs generously with salt and pepper on both sides.
3. Place chicken in a vacuum-seal bag or zipper-lock bag with herbs and garlic if using.
4. Remove air using vacuum sealer or water displacement method.
5. Submerge bag in water bath ensuring chicken is fully underwater. Cook for 4 to 8 hours for pull-off-the-bone tenderness.
6. Remove bag from water and transfer chicken to an ice bath for 10 minutes (optional, for easier handling).
7. Remove chicken from bag, reserving any gelled juices for sauce if desired. Pat chicken completely dry with paper towels.
8. Press chicken skin-side down on a cutting board to flatten the skin against the meat.
9. Heat oil in a nonstick or cast iron skillet over medium heat. Place chicken skin-side down and hold flat with a spatula.
10. Cook until skin is golden brown and crisp, about 8 minutes. Flip and warm second side for 2 minutes.
11. Rest for 2 minutes before serving.

---

## Time and Temperature

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Temperature | 165°F / 74°C |
| Time | 4-8 hours |
| Difficulty | 2/5 |

---

## Why Sous Vide Works Here

Chicken thighs contain more collagen and fat than breasts, which means they benefit from longer cooking times that break down connective tissue without drying out. Sous vide allows you to hold the meat in the sweet spot where collagen converts to gelatin while the meat stays moist. The result is dark meat that ranges from sliceable to shreddable depending on your chosen cook time.

---

## Tips

- Cook 1-4 hours at 165°F for juicy, sliceable thighs; cook 4-8 hours for pull-off-the-bone tenderness
- Chilling the chicken in an ice bath after sous vide makes it easier to handle and crisp the skin
- Save the gelled juices from the bag to make a quick pan sauce with wine, mustard, and butter
- Bone-in thighs need the full cooking time for collagen breakdown; boneless thighs can be done in 1-4 hours
- For extra-crispy skin, place seared chicken under a hot broiler for 1-2 minutes after pan-searing
