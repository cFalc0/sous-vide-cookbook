# Chicken Breast

Sous vide chicken breast delivers unparalleled juiciness by holding the meat at a precise temperature that prevents the dryness common with traditional methods. This technique transforms what is often the most challenging cut of poultry into consistently tender, flavorful results every time.

---

## Ingredients

- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (6-8 ounces each)
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme or rosemary (optional)
- 1 tablespoon olive oil or butter (for searing)

---

## Steps

1. Preheat water bath to 145°F (63°C) using a sous vide circulator.
2. Season chicken breasts generously with salt and pepper on both sides.
3. Place chicken in a vacuum-seal bag or zipper-lock bag with herb sprigs if using.
4. Remove air using vacuum sealer or water displacement method: seal bag almost completely, then lower into water to force air out through the small opening, and seal fully.
5. Submerge bag in water bath ensuring chicken is fully underwater. Cook for 1.5 to 4 hours.
6. Remove bag from water and take out chicken. Pat chicken completely dry with paper towels.
7. Heat oil or butter in a cast iron or stainless steel skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
8. Sear chicken for 1-2 minutes per side until golden brown and crispy.
9. Let rest for 2 minutes, then slice and serve immediately.

---

## Time and Temperature

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Temperature | 145°F / 63°C |
| Time | 1.5-4 hours |
| Difficulty | 2/5 |

---

## Why Sous Vide Works Here

Chicken breast is lean and unforgiving with traditional cooking methods, quickly becoming dry when it passes 160°F. Sous vide holds the meat at exactly the target temperature, so the proteins never overcoagulate and expel their juices. The result is chicken that is evenly cooked from edge to center with maximum moisture retention.

---

## Tips

- Thicker breasts (over 1.5 inches) benefit from the full 4 hours for pasteurization; thinner breasts need only 1.5 hours
- Pat the chicken bone-dry before searing to get a good crust without steaming the meat
- For meal prep, cook the chicken and store in the refrigerator for up to 4 days before searing
- To reheat cooked chicken breast, sous vide at 140°F for 45 minutes to 1 hour
- Frozen chicken breasts can be cooked directly from frozen by adding 30 minutes to the cook time
