# Breakfast Sausage

Juicy, evenly cooked sausage patties that stay moist throughout. Sous vide at 150°F ensures food safety while preserving the texture that pan-frying often overcooks.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 lb breakfast sausage meat (pork or turkey)
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- 1/2 tsp sage
- 1/2 tsp thyme
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil (for searing)

---

## Steps

1. Mix sausage meat with maple syrup, sage, thyme, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
2. Form into 2-ounce patties, about 1/2 inch thick.
3. Place patties in a zipper-lock bag in a single layer.
4. Seal using water displacement method.
5. Preheat water bath to 150°F (66°C) and submerge bag.
6. Cook for 1-2 hours.
7. Remove patties, pat dry with paper towels.
8. Heat oil in skillet over medium-high heat.
9. Sear patties 1 minute per side until browned.
10. Serve immediately with eggs and toast.

---

## Time and Temperature

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Temperature | 150°F / 66°C |
| Time | 1-2 hrs |
| Difficulty | 2/5 |

---

## Why Sous Vide Works Here

Breakfast sausage often overcooks in the skillet, turning grey and dry. Sous vide holds the meat at exactly 150°F, pasteurizing while keeping the interior juicy and flavorful. The quick sear only adds color and texture.

---

## Tips

- Mix seasonings the night before for deeper flavor
- Cook sausage patties alongside poached eggs in the same water bath
- Hold at temperature for up to 4 hours if needed
- Freeze raw patties for quick weekday breakfasts
- Add a pinch of red pepper flakes for heat
