## Why Sous Vide for Breakfast

Morning cooking often happens when you're rushed or not fully awake. Sous vide eliminates the timing stress:

- **Eggs** — Impossible to overcook at 145°F
- **Meats** — Hold at temp while you prep other items
- **Vegetables** — Ready when you are, not the other way around

---

## Tips

- Start your water bath before prepping other items
- Most breakfast items can hold for 30+ minutes after target time
- Use the same water bath for multiple breakfast components
- Prep ingredients night before for zero-morning effort
