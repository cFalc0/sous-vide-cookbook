# Poached Eggs

The perfect poached egg with jammy yolk and set whites, every single time. Sous vide at 145°F eliminates the guesswork and timing stress of traditional poaching.

---

## Ingredients

- 4-6 large eggs (cold from refrigerator)
- 1 tbsp white vinegar (optional, for traditional poaching)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Toast or English muffins for serving

---

## Steps

1. Preheat sous vide water bath to 145°F (63°C).
2. Gently lower cold eggs (in shell) directly into the water bath.
3. Cook for 45-60 minutes — the longer time yields firmer whites.
4. Remove eggs from water and crack into a small bowl.
5. Serve immediately over toast, or transfer to ice bath for later use.
6. Season with salt and pepper.

---

## Time and Temperature

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Temperature | 145°F / 63°C |
| Time | 45-60 min |
| Difficulty | 1/5 |

---

## Why Sous Vide Works Here

Eggs are the precision showcase of sous vide. One degree changes the result dramatically. Traditional poaching requires perfect timing and swirling water. Sous vide holds the eggs at exactly 145°F, delivering jammy yolks and set whites with zero babysitting.

---

## Tips

- 45 minutes = runnier yolk, 60 minutes = firmer yolk
- Cook eggs in shell directly — no bag needed
- Chill in ice bath after cooking for up to 48 hours refrigerated
- Reheat cold poached eggs in 145°F water for 10 minutes
- For 63°C eggs (classic jammy), cook at 145°F for exactly 60 minutes
