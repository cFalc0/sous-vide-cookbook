# Immersion Circulator

An immersion circulator is a compact countertop device that heats and circulates water in a container to maintain a precise, constant temperature for sous vide cooking, enabling perfectly cooked food with restaurant-quality consistency from the comfort of home.

## Summary

An immersion circulator—sometimes called a sous vide stick or precision cooker—is the core equipment that makes sous vide cooking possible. It clamps onto any heat-safe container, heats water to a precise temperature, and circulates it to eliminate hot and cold spots. Modern devices connect to smartphone apps via WiFi or Bluetooth for remote monitoring and control. The market is led by Anova and Breville (Joule), ranging from budget-friendly ($70) to premium ($400) models.

## Details

### How Immersion Circulators Work

The device heats water to a specific temperature and keeps it there through a combination of:
- **Heating element** (750-1200 watts) to bring water to target temp
- **Pump/impeller** to circulate water and eliminate temperature variation
- **PID controller** to maintain temperature within 0.1-0.3°F of set point

This precision is what makes sous vide possible—food can cook for hours without risk of overcooking as long as temperature remains stable.

### Popular Models

**Anova Precision Cooker Nano** — Best budget option ($70-100)
- 850 watts, Bluetooth, holds temp within 0.2°F
- Compact (12.8 inches tall), 1.7 lbs
- Best for containers up to 5 gallons
- Ideal entry point for beginners

**Anova Precision Cooker 3.0** — Best overall ($150-230)
- 1100 watts, dual-band WiFi
- Heats 5 gallons to 140°F in ~20 minutes
- On-device controls + app, adjustable clamp
- Best balance of power, features, price

**Breville Joule Turbo** — Best premium ($250)
- 1100 watts, app-only control (no on-device screen)
- Magnetic base, Turbo mode for faster cooking
- Most compact design
- Tech-savvy cooks who prefer app-only experience

**Anova Precision Cooker Pro** — Best for heavy use ($400)
- 1200 watts, IPX7 waterproof
- Stainless steel, 12 L/min flow rate
- For containers up to 26 gallons
- Professional-grade durability

### Key Features to Consider

| Feature | Why It Matters |
|---------|---------------|
| Wattage | Higher = faster heating, better for large containers |
| Temperature accuracy | Within 0.2°F is standard for quality models |
| Connectivity | WiFi for remote control; Bluetooth for nearby |
| On-device controls | Backup when phone isn't available |
| Flow rate | Higher = better circulation in large vessels |

### Choosing the Right Circulator

**Beginners:** Anova Nano — accessible price, reliable performance
**Most home cooks:** Anova Precision Cooker 3.0 — best overall value
**Tech enthusiasts:** Breville Joule Turbo — premium app experience
**Frequent/heavy use:** Anova Pro — professional durability
