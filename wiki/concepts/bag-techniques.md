# Bag Techniques

Proper bag sealing ensures even heat transfer and prevents floating. The water displacement method lets home cooks achieve professional results without special equipment.

---

## Water Displacement Method

1. Place food in a freezer-grade zipper-lock bag
2. Seal 80% — leave a 1–2 inch opening at one corner
3. Slowly lower into water, keeping opening above the water line
4. Water pressure pushes air out through the opening
5. Just before water reaches opening, seal the rest

**Tip:** Use Ziploc Freezer bags — BPA-free polyethylene that withstands sous vide temps.

---

## Preventing Floating

Bags float when air remains inside. Solutions:

- Add stainless steel weights to the bag
- Use binder clips to clip the bag to the container edge
- Double-bag for long cooks

---

## Vacuum Sealers vs. Zip Bags

| Method | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| Zip bags + water displacement | Cheap, no equipment | Not true vacuum |
| Vacuum sealer | Complete air removal, best for long cooks | Machine costs $50–200 |

**When to use vacuum bags:**
- Cooks over 4–6 hours
- Temperatures above 158°F
- Best heat transfer needed
