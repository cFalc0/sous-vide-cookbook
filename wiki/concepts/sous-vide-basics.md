# Sous Vide Basics

Cooking food sealed in bags submerged in precisely temperature-controlled water. The result: edge-to-edge perfect doneness that's impossible with traditionalmethods.

---

## How It Works

1. Season food and seal in a bag
2. Submerge in a water bath精确 at target temperature
3. Cook for-set time (can be hours)
4. Remove and sear for crust

**The key:** Water temperature = final internal temperature. No risk of overcooking.

---

## Equipment Needed

- Immersion circulator ($70+)
- Container (pot, cooler, dedicated)
- Bags (freezer-grade zip lock work fine)

---

## Advantages

- **Exact doneness** — edge-to-edge, no gradient
- **Impossible to overcook** — temp stays stable
- **Consistent results** — every time
- **Hands-off** — no flipping, monitoring
- **Tough cuts become tender** — 24–72 hour cooks

---

## The Basic Workflow

1. Set circulator to target temperature
2. Seal food in bag with water displacement method
3. Submerge — ensure fully underwater
4. Set timer
5. When done: remove, pat dry, sear
6. Rest 2–5 minutes, serve

---

## Food Safety

- Keep water bath above 130°F for extended cooks
- Below 130°F: max 4 hours total
- Ice bath and refrigerate promptly if not serving
- Never reuse marinade raw
