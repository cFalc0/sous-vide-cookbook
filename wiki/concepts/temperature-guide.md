# Temperature Guide

Sous vide gives you precise doneness control. The water bath temperature becomes the final internal temperature of your food.

---

## Beef (Steaks)

| Doneness | Temp (°F) | Temp (°C) |
|---------|----------|----------|
| Rare | 120–128 | 49–53 |
| Medium-rare | 129–134 | 54–57 |
| Medium | 135–144 | 57–62 |
| Medium-well | 145–155 | 63–68 |
| Well done | 156+ | 69+ |

**Note:** Medium-rare at 131°F is the most popular. FDA says beef at 130°F+ for 112+ min is safe.

---

## Pork

| Cut | Temp (°F) | Time |
|-----|-----------|------|
| Tenderloin | 140 | 1–4 hrs |
| Chops | 140 | 1–4 hrs |
| Shoulder | 165 | 12–18 hrs |
| Ribs | 165 | 4–6 hrs |

**Note:** USDA recommends 145°F for fresh pork.

---

## Poultry

| Cut | Temp (°F) | Texture |
|-----|-----------|--------|
| Chicken breast | 140–145 | Soft, juicy |
| Chicken breast | 150 | Tender, slightly stringy |
| Chicken breast | 160 | Firm, traditional |
| Chicken thighs | 148–155 | Rich, tender |
| Thighs (shreddable) | 165 | Fall-off-bone |
| Turkey breast | 141 | Ideal balance |

**Note:** 140°F chicken breast is pasteurized in ~2 hours. Below 130°F limit to 4 hours max.

---

## Fish

| Style | Temp (°F) | Time |
|-------|-----------|------|
| Sashimi-style | 104–110 | 30 min |
| Translucent | 120 | 30–45 min |
| Fully cooked | 132 | 30–45 min |
| Firm (traditional) | 140 | 20–30 min |

- **Salmon:** 115°F for buttery-smooth, 130°F for firm and flaky
- **White fish (cod, halibut):** 130°F ideal

---

## Eggs

| Style | Temp (°C) | Temp (°F) |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| 63°C egg (jammy yolk) | 63 | 145 |
| Soft-boiled | 64–65 | 147–149 |
| Hard-boiled | 71–74 | 160–165 |

**Note:** 45–60 minutes minimum for thermal equilibrium. 1°F makes a noticeable difference.

---

## Quick Reference

- **Below 130°F:** Max 4 hours (food safety rule)
- **130°F+:** Safe for extended cooks
- **Thickness matters:** Add time for thickness over 1.5 inches
