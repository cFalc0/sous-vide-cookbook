# Timing Guide

Sous vide gives you precise control over cooking duration—but this flexibility comes with rules. Timing depends on whether you're cooking for doneness, pasteurization, or tenderization. Understanding these three phases is the key to consistent results.

---

## The Three Goals

Every cook addresses all three, but emphasis changes by protein:

1. **Doneness** — Getting the center to target temperature. Takes 30 min to 3 hours depending on thickness.

2. **Pasteurization** — Eliminating harmful bacteria. Requires specific time-temperature combinations.

3. **Tenderization** — Breaking down collagen into gelatin. This is the sous vide superpower: tough cuts become fork-tender with a pink center.

---

## The 4-Hour Rule

The USDA states food must not remain in the temperature danger zone (40°F–130°F) for more than 4 hours total.

- **Below 130°F**: Maximum 4 hours. Applies to rare steak, very rare fish.
- **130°F and above**: Safe for extended periods (24–72 hours).

This is why beef tenderloin at 120°F is limited to 2–4 hours, while short ribs at 130°F+ can go 48–72 hours safely.

---

## The 24-Hour Rule

Beyond 24 hours, texture changes significantly:
- Collagen converts to gelatin at 160°F+ (6–8 hours)
- Muscle fibers soften at 130°F+ (24–48 hours)
- Beef short ribs: 48 hours minimum for fork-tender

---

## Timing by Protein

| Protein | Time | Notes |
|---------|------|-------|
| Beef tenderloin | 1–4 hours | Rare to medium only |
| Beef ribeye | 2–4 hours | Medium-rare ideal |
| Beef short ribs | 24–72 hours | Fork-tender |
| Pork tenderloin | 1–4 hours | 140°F for juicy |
| Pork shoulder | 12–24 hours | Pulled texture |
| Chicken breast | 1–4 hours | 140–145°F, not 165°F |
| Chicken thighs | 4–8 hours | Dark meat needs longer |
| Salmon | 30–45 minutes | Delicate, overcooks easily |
| White fish | 30–45 minutes | 130°F ideal |
| Eggs | 45–90 minutes | 63°C = jammy yolk |

---

## Thickness Adjustment

Time is governed by thickness, not weight:

- **1 inch thick**: Add 30 min to minimum time
- **1.5 inches**: Add 1 hour
- **2 inches**: Add 1.5 hours
